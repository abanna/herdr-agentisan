# ADR-001 A45. Boss notices from the GitHub collector

*Adds to:* A1, A29 and A44. Recorded with NERD-5326 (G8 of the parity inventory), in two parts: part a, and part b's reviews, each with its P-badge counts.

- **What is told.** After each successful open-PR list of a project's repo, the collector diffs it against `state.db` and tells the project's boss, through A44's Batcher and sink:
  - `merged`, keyed by the merge commit. A PR that left the list is looked up with `gh pr view <n> --repo=<repo> --json=state,mergeCommit`. A closed PR leaves silently. One still open, or a lookup that failed, is looked up again the next interval.
    - At most 10 lookups per repo per collection, lowest number first.
    - A list that comes back at its limit of 100, counted before release-please's are skipped, may be cut off, so it looks nothing up.
    - No merged list is read: `gh pr list --state merged` sorts by creation, so a long-lived PR merged now falls past any small limit. So a PR opened and merged between two successful collections, the daemon running or not, is never in the open set, and its merge is not told.
  - `ci_failed` and `ci_passed` (A29's CI rule), and `dirty`, a merge conflict, each keyed by the head commit. A new push can fail or conflict again. A rerun that fails again on a head already told sends nothing; that gap is accepted, since a fix is a new push.
  - `approved`, the first 👍, once per PR.
  - `review` (part b), a reviewer's new review, keyed by its reviewer's login and the time it was submitted: `latestReviews` gives no id and no commit. A re-review at the same head is a new review.
    - Each new one, neither told nor held, is looked up for its counts with one GraphQL call: the reviewer's last 5 reviews of the PR, under its login and, since the author filter needs it for a bot (GraphQL's login has none), its login with `[bot]`. It is matched by login and time.
    - The counts are every `![P<n> Badge](` image, P0 to P3, in the review's body and in its inline comments that start a thread: Codex puts its findings in the comments, or, with none there, in the body. A reply may quote one, so replies are not counted. Of more than 100 comments, the line says only the first 100 were read.
    - A lookup that fails is retried each interval. An answer without the review tells it without counts, since asking again would not find it. A login that is not one, and a review its project skips, are handed over with no lookup.
    - **Not told:** the PR author's own reviews, which `latestReviews` leaves out (on our repos, every PR is the user's, and the team's "fixed in" replies are the author's), and a reviewer's earlier review when two land inside one interval or one downtime, since `latestReviews` keeps one per reviewer.
- **The fields.** A29's call gains `headRefOid` and `latestReviews`. The header and snapshot v1 are unchanged.
- **Once per event (A1, migration 0004).** `github_seen` holds every event told, by project, repo, PR, kind and key; `github_open` the open PRs of the last successful collection; `github_seeded` the repos whose first collection was recorded. Keys carry the project as well as the repo, so two projects on one repo are each told.
  - **The seed.** A repo's first successful collection records what is true then and tells nothing, so G8 does not tell every open PR's history the day it ships. The mark keeps a later restart from seeding again.
  - **A seed per kind (migration 0006).** A kind added later, `review` the first, has its own mark in `github_seeded_kinds`: in a repo already seeded, its first collection records what is true of it and tells none of it, nor looks any of it up, while the other kinds are told as before. Each successful collection observes the repo's marks again, so a repo still collected never loses one to the prune and seeds again.
  - **Recorded when told.** An event is recorded in its notice's acknowledgement (A44), once the sink accepted its message or its kind was skipped; a merge resolves its PR in the same transaction. A refused message, the size cut, a failed write, or a restart before the flush records nothing, and the event is told again: a duplicate, never a loss. A notice handed over is held for its project's `gap` plus one interval, so it is not handed twice meanwhile.
  - **Restarts.** What happened while the daemon was down is told at its first collection, including the merge of a PR that was open at its last one.
  - **Failures.** A failed list tells nothing and changes nothing, so it never reads as every PR closing.
  - **Pruning.** A row the collector has not observed for 30 days goes in the daily prune. An open PR's rows are observed at each collection; a merged PR's go 30 days after the merge, and a repo no longer configured ages out.
- **No GitHub free text.** The lines carry PR numbers, 7-character oids, counts and the repo name from the configuration, never a title, branch, body or comment: anyone who can open a PR on a public repo would otherwise type into the boss's session. `herdr-agentisan #50 merged as 1e0fb1b`, `#50 CI failed at 1e0fb1b (2 of 7 checks)`, `#50 CI passed at 1e0fb1b`, `#50 has a merge conflict at 1e0fb1b`, `#50 got a 👍`. A review's line has the login, checked as one, else "a reviewer"; the state as a word; and the counts, P0 first and none at zero: `#1006 review by chatgpt-codex-connector at afa239d: commented, P1 ×2`, `#50 review by alice at 1e0fb1b: approved, no P-badges`, `#50 review by alice: commented, P-badges not read`.
- **Pace and configuration.** The six kinds are paced per project like A44's, but never dropped as repeats: the seen-set tells each once, and CI failing again at a new head inside `repeat` is news. `notices.skip` can name each; a skipped event is recorded all the same, so a boss that stops skipping a kind is not told the old ones.
- **The goroutines.** The collector runs the gh calls and the diff on its own goroutine, after the header's two calls, so a slow lookup never delays that repo's header. It does delay the repos collected after it, and the next collection: a repo's lookups, the merges first and then the reviews, share up to 10 gh calls of up to 15 s each. Meanwhile the header keeps each repo's last good state for 5 intervals. It hands the notices to the team poll through a queue; the poll adds them to its Batcher before each flush, and the acknowledgements run there.
- **Cutover (D2).** boss-pr-watch-v3.sh, a second GitHub poller beside the daemon's, retires once a boss following the log has seen a shift's merges, CI, 👍 and Codex reviews, with their P-badges, match its own.
