# ADR-001 A58. A gpt- model's short name carries its version

*Overrides:* A38's "Short model names" sentences on gpt- and Codex models. *Adds to:* A39 and A56, whose model the column draws. Recorded with NERD-5504 (Codex parity R22 and R23, part of NERD-5501), from area B of the parity audit: `gpt-5.5` drew `gpt-`, and `gpt-6-sol`, `gpt-6.1-sol` and `gpt-5.6-sol` all drew `sol`. So a worker routing launched on `codex_strong` and one started by hand on another sol model looked alike. A38's text is kept as written.

- **A gpt- model is its word and its version,** at most six cells. The word and version come from the name after `gpt-`:
  - `cdx` and what comes before `codex`, when the name says codex: `gpt-5.5-codex` is `cdx5.5`. What follows codex is not drawn: `gpt-5.1-codex-max` is `cdx5.1`, as `gpt-5.1-codex` is. No routing binding is such a variant.
  - Otherwise the last part, when it is letters only, and what comes before it: `gpt-6.1-sol` is `sol6.1`, `gpt-6-luna` `luna6`, `gpt-6-astra` `astra6`.
  - Otherwise `gpt` and all of it: `gpt-5.5` is `gpt5.5`.
- **The version is what tells two models of one word apart.** A label too wide for six cells loses its word first, down to one cell (`gpt-4.1-mini` is `min4.1`), and its version only then. A wide character does not fit one cell, so beside a five-cell version a word that starts with one goes whole (`gpt-10.25-日本` is `10.25`).
- **Unchanged:**
  - Claude's families are `opus`, `son`, `hai` and `fab`, with no version. Every Claude binding of the routing rules is a different family.
  - Any other model whose name says codex is `cdx`.
  - Any other model is its first word, cut to four cells.
- **The column is as wide as its widest label,** so it is six cells only on a team that runs a gpt- model.
  - The extra cells, two at most, come out of the stage, which takes what is left. Every other column keeps its width; the ctx and item columns sit those cells further right.
  - On the user's 97-cell portrait pane every column stays, even with every name and item at its cap, and so at 120 (`TestTheModelColumnWidensIntoTheStage`, `TestTheModelColumnAtTheCaps`).
  - At 80, a team whose names and items are at their caps has no two cells to spare: its time column, the first the renderer drops, gives way, where a team of four-cell models keeps it.
  - A group view's box draws the same label.
- **Every routing binding draws a label of its own** that fits the column, checked against the rules file the binary embeds (`TestShortModelTellsTheBindingsApart`). So a binding added later is held to it too.
