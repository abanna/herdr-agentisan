# ADR-001 A65. A Codex spawn reads its composer

*Refines:* A23's readiness rule, under which a Codex fails with a hint naming its hook review and its directory-trust prompt, and so does one whose screen shows the hook review, and A43's "Spawn (part 4)". A23's and A43's text is kept as written. Recorded with NERD-5513 (Codex parity P11, rows R33 and R11), from the user's Q21 and the boss's answers of 2026-10-23.

- **Why.** herdr reports a new Codex ready on any startup screen its manifest does not name. With no rule matched, the screen reads idle, and after herdr's 3 s settle the agent is interactive (herdr 0.9.5 `src/terminal/state.rs:2259-2264`, `src/app/agents.rs:10`). Spawn's only Codex check was a 500 ms read for the hook review, so a Codex at a login screen or another startup notice looked ready, and its first prompt was typed into that screen.
- **The composer read.** Once herdr says a Codex worker is ready, spawn waits up to 1.5 s, in one `pane.wait_for_output`, for a line of its screen that shows Codex's empty composer, `Ask Codex to do anything` (Codex 0.163.0 `tui/src/chatwidget.rs:2079`), or the hook review's `Hooks need review` or `Trust all and continue`.
  - herdr answers the first matching line from the top. The review clears the whole screen (`tui/src/startup_hooks_review.rs:304-307`), so it never shares the screen with the composer.
  - **The composer:** spawn goes on. A Codex that started well shows it before herdr's ready, so the read answers at once.
  - **The hook review:** the spawn fails at once, "is at Codex's hook review", with the hint.
  - **Neither, within 1.5 s:** the spawn fails, "never showed Codex's composer", with the hint.
  - **A failed read** is herdr's (`herdr_failed`), or out of time when the op's deadline ends it, as before.
  - **A Claude worker** gets no read. The read follows the launch's kind, so any Codex worker gets it, whatever its type (A59).
- **The hook-review read stays.** After the composer read, spawn reads the screen for the hook review alone for 500 ms, as before.
  - Codex 0.163.0 shows a startup draft first. It is an editable composer with the same placeholder (`tui/src/startup_draft.rs:520`), and the hook review comes after it (`tui/src/lib.rs:2073`).
  - No line tells the draft from the real composer. The configured draft shows the header, the directory, the placeholder and `? for shortcuts`. The status line and terminal title that only the real composer shows are the user's to change or switch off.
  - So the composer read alone would accept the draft at once and miss a review that follows it, which the 500 ms read catches.
  - The user's Q21 chose the composer read "replacing" the 500 ms read. It rested on live check I's timings, which measured the draft: the check's `model: loading` test could never match, since 0.163.0 draws no such line. Keeping both reads adds protection and costs nothing on a healthy spawn.
  - **The residual.** The 500 ms hook-only watch starts after the positive composer read, not at herdr's ready. A hook review arriving after that watch ends can still be missed. A directory-trust prompt or another non-hook startup blocker appearing after the draft can also be missed, even during that watch: neither matches its hook-review pattern. herdr's settle is a not-before delay, not an upper bound on Codex bootstrap or menu arrival. Historical check B recorded the review 1.7 s before herdr's ready at that machine's load; it does not establish live acceptance of this residual. The live-check row under load remains pending; the positive read followed by the 500 ms hook read is unchanged.
- **The hint** names Codex's three startup screens and the step past all three: "Codex may be at its update prompt, its hook review or its directory-trust prompt; start `codex --no-daemon` once in a herdr pane in the repo, choose "Skip until next version" (or update Codex), trust the hooks and the folder, then spawn again".
  - **When Codex shows its startup update prompt,** at 0.163.0 it reads `Update available · <current> → <latest>`, with Update now, Skip and Skip until next version (`tui/src/update_prompt.rs:205-260`). herdr 0.9.5 reads it as blocked (`src/detect/manifests/codex.toml:71-74`), so a spawn at that prompt fails "blocked during startup". The prompt is conditional: startup update checks must be enabled, source-build versions suppress it, and an upgrade action must be available (`tui/src/updates.rs:152-167`, `tui/src/update_prompt.rs:55-60`).
  - **"Skip until next version" asks Codex to remember the dismissal** (`tui/src/update_prompt.rs:95-99`). Esc and Skip skip one start.
  - **Why one hint for all three.** Neither a blocked start nor a missing composer can say which screen it is: `agent.get` names no rule.
